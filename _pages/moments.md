<h2 id="sdc">SDC Software Development Club, NYCU · Aug 2025 – Present</h2>

<div class="t-sub"><i>Treasurer (財務)</i></div>

I have served as Treasurer of the NYCU Software Development Club (SDC) since August 2025.

SDC has shown me many more possibilities. Our officers and members come not only from NYCU but also from NTHU, NTUST and other schools, all sharing a passion for software development, technology and the club itself, and here I have met many people with strong hands-on engineering skills. At **Intern Corner**, I also saw how strong the club's network is: many alumni, including former officers and members, stay in touch and come back to share their internship and full-time experiences, which lets us understand the workplace and industry trends early.

<h2 id="sitcon-2026">SITCON 2026 · Mar 2026</h2>

<div class="t-sub"><i>Students' Information Technology Conference (學生計算機年會) · Venue Team (場務組) · Academia Sinica</i></div>

Through SDC members, I learned that there is a technology conference organized entirely by students. As a member of the Venue Team, I helped with attendee check-in, on-site support, catering and logistics. Working on the organizing side, I saw for the first time that behind a smoothly run event and live stream there is a very united organizing team, and I came to understand the ongoing discussion and inspiration that drive the SITCON community.

<h2 id="nepal-2026">International Volunteering in Nepal · Jan 2026</h2>

<div class="t-sub"><i>VYA Vision Youth Action (願景青年行動網協會) · Nepal NL89 volunteer team</i></div>

In January 2026 I joined an international volunteer team at a school in Nepal. Together with my teammates, I planned and taught lessons in English, including a class on **friendship, respect and feelings**, a two-session unit on **gender equality and personal boundaries**, and a class called **"The Cost of Chaos"** on noticing stress and handling emotions. Outside the classroom we also ran activities such as a cleanliness contest.

Beyond studying, I believe it is important to set foot in the world myself. Working with the children at a mountain school also helped me understand what I want to pursue in the future. From the children's happy smiles to how seriously they worked during our lessons, the experience was deeply rewarding.
