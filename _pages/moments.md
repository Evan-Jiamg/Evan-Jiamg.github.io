<h2 id="nepal-2026">International Volunteering in Nepal · Jan 2026</h2>

<div class="t-sub"><i>VYA Vision Youth Action (願景青年行動網協會) · Nepal NL89 volunteer team</i></div>

In January 2026 I joined an international volunteer team at a school in Nepal. Together with my teammates, I planned and taught lessons in English, including a class on **friendship, respect and feelings**, a two-session unit on **gender equality and personal boundaries**, and a class called **"The Cost of Chaos"** on noticing stress and handling emotions. Outside the classroom we also ran activities such as a cleanliness contest.
