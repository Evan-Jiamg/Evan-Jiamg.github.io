\section{Undergraduate Research Project}
\hspace*{0.8em}{\headfont\bfseries Hybrid Coevolutionary Opinion Games (H-COG)}\ \ \href{https://github.com/Evan-Jiamg/Echo-Chamber-Simulation}{\textcolor{linkblue}{\faGithub\ Code \& Data}}\hfill{\itshape Feb 2026 -- Present}\\[0.2em]
\hspace*{0.8em}$\rightarrow$ {\bfseries 1st Place}\ ·\ ORSTW Undergraduate Project Competition\ ·\ AI and Big Data Analytics Division\hfill{\itshape Jun 2026}\\[0.2em]
\hspace*{0.8em}$\rightarrow$ {\bfseries Co-author}\ ·\ {\bfseries Accepted}\ ·\ Taiwan Summer Workshop on IM (TSWIM) 2026 Research Incubator\hfill{\itshape May 2026}\\[0.2em]
\hspace*{0.8em}{\itshape Economics and Computing Lab, NYCU, Advisor: Prof. Po-An Chen}
\begin{bullets}
  \item Proposed a hybrid framework that places classical Friedkin--Johnsen agents and LLM agents with language reasoning in the same dynamically rewired opinion-game network, filling two gaps: opinion games lack language reasoning, and LLM simulations do not measure social efficiency. Accepted at CSoNet 2026 as first author.
  \item Initialized agent stances from real Reddit data and ran 540 simulations. LLM agents reach consensus most easily, but at the highest overall social cost, about five times that of a classical FJ network; echo chambers form because of the dynamic rewiring rule, not the initial network structure.
\end{bullets}
